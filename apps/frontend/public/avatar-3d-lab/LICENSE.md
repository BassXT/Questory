# Quaternius avatar lab assets

The two models in this directory originate from the Quaternius
`Ultimate Modular Characters` pack:

- `quaternius-adventurer.gltf`
- `quaternius-casual.gltf`

Author: Quaternius  
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html  
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)

The files are stored locally and are used only by Questory's isolated 3D avatar
prototype. They do not require a remote runtime service.
